01 / THE BREW GUIDE

COFFEE, AT HOME6 MIN READ

A better cup begins before the first sip.

The little choices matter: freshly ground beans, water just off the boil, and a few quiet minutes to let the coffee bloom. Our baristas share a simple, repeatable way to bring a balanced cup into your own kitchen.

Start with freshness

Choose whole beans and grind only what you need. Keep them sealed away from heat and sunlight, then use a consistent ratio of coffee to water. A kitchen scale helps, but a steady routine matters most.

Give the brew room

Pour slowly and evenly, wetting all the grounds. Let the coffee bloom briefly before continuing. If the cup tastes sharp, grind a little finer or extend the brew; if it tastes heavy or bitter, try a coarser grind.

Good coffee is less about expensive gear and more about paying attention to what’s in your cup.

Behind every bean, a pair of hands.

Our coffee starts long before it reaches the grinder. Meet the growers and local suppliers whose care shapes the flavor, quality, and character of every cup.

Quality has a source

We look for thoughtful growing practices, careful harvesting, and partners who can tell us exactly where their beans come from. Knowing the people behind our ingredients helps us serve them with the respect they deserve.

A great cup connects the person who grew it to the person enjoying it.
